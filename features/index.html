Shared team inbox for WhatsApp

Stop passing one phone around. Every WhatsApp conversation — plus Facebook and Instagram messages — comes into one inbox that your whole team can use at the same time.

  • Assign chats and see who is handling what
  • Private notes for your team only
  • Unread, Mine, Unassigned and Archived filters
  • Search chats, contacts and messages

Rule-based chatbot builder

Design the conversation once and let ChatsSync answer for you. Customers tap buttons and lists, the bot replies with your approved answers, and a human takes over when it matters.

  • Drag-and-drop flow canvas
  • Text, buttons, lists, media, links and questions
  • Conditions, delays and labels
  • Hand over to an agent at any step

Multiple bots for different jobs

Run separate bots for appointments, price enquiries, after-hours replies or order tracking, and publish or pause each one whenever you like.

  • Draft and published states
  • One bot per inbox or use case
  • Test before you publish

Bulk WhatsApp campaigns

Send offers, payment reminders and updates to the right customers using Meta-approved message templates — the official way to message in bulk.

  • Choose audience by label or segment
  • Send now or schedule
  • Sent, delivered, read and replied stats
  • Failed messages shown clearly

WhatsApp template builder

Contacts, labels and segments

Every customer gets a profile with their details, labels and full history, so your team always has the context to close the sale.
