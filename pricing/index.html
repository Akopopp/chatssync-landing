Pricing FAQ

Billing questions

What is the difference between the plans?

Every plan includes the AI agent, chatbot builder, appointments and catalog. Plans differ in AI replies, contacts, team members, Facebook and Instagram accounts, broadcasts, chatbot flows and AI FAQs. Pro also includes setup done for you.

What happens when my AI replies run out?

You can add 3,000 more AI replies for Rs 1,000, or move to a bigger plan. Your team can keep replying in the inbox as usual.

Can I add more WhatsApp numbers or team members?

Yes. An extra WhatsApp number is Rs 1,500 a month and an extra team member is Rs 500 a month.

Is there a setup fee?

Yes, a one-time setup fee of Rs 2,000.

Are Meta’s WhatsApp charges included?

No. Meta charges its own fees for some WhatsApp template messages (such as marketing messages). Those are separate from the ChatsSync plan price. We explain how they work before you start.

How do I pay?

Message us on WhatsApp at 0322 8413450 and we will share the payment details for your plan.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. You can move to a bigger plan or a longer period whenever you like.

Do you offer pricing outside Pakistan?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp and we will share pricing in your currency.

Not sure which plan to choose?

Message us on WhatsApp — tell us about your business and we will recommend the right plan.
