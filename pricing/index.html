Pricing FAQ

Billing questions

Do all plans include the same features?

Yes. Every plan includes the full ChatsSync platform and 1 WhatsApp number. Longer plans simply cost less per month.

Can I add more WhatsApp numbers?

Yes. Each plan includes 1 WhatsApp number. Extra numbers are charged separately and the price depends on your needs — message us for a quote.

Are Meta’s WhatsApp fees included?

No. Meta charges its own fees for some WhatsApp template messages (such as marketing messages). Those are separate from the ChatsSync plan price. We explain how they work before you start.
