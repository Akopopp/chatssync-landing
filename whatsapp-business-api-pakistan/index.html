What is the WhatsApp Business API?

The WhatsApp Business API (officially the WhatsApp Business Platform by Meta) lets businesses message customers on WhatsApp at scale. Unlike the normal app on one phone, the API connects your WhatsApp number to software, so many team members, chatbots and campaigns can work on the same number.

Meta does not provide a chat screen for the API. You use it through a platform such as ChatsSync, which gives your team an inbox, a chatbot builder, campaigns and reports.

What you need to use the WhatsApp API in Pakistan

  1. A Meta (Facebook) Business account for your business.
  2. A phone number that can receive an SMS or call for verification.
  3. Business details such as your business name, website or Facebook page — these help with business verification.
  4. A WhatsApp API platform such as ChatsSync to manage chats, bots and campaigns.

Message templates and the 24-hour window

When a customer messages you, a 24-hour customer service window opens. Inside it you can reply freely with any message — text, images, documents or chatbot replies.

To message a customer first, or after the 24 hours have passed, you must use a message template approved by Meta. Templates have categories such as marketing (offers and promotions), utility (order updates, reminders) and authentication (one-time codes).

ChatsSync shows when the window has closed and lets you pick an approved template in one click. Its template builder previews the message exactly as the customer will see it.

How Meta charges for the WhatsApp API

Meta charges businesses per delivered template message, with the price depending on the template category and the customer’s country. Replies inside the 24-hour customer service window are free. Meta updates its rates from time to time, so check Meta’s official WhatsApp Business pricing page for the latest numbers.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use the WhatsApp Business API in Pakistan?

Yes. Businesses in Pakistan can use Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform. You need a Meta Business account, a phone number for verification and a provider or software such as ChatsSync to manage your chats.

Do I need a website to get the WhatsApp API?

A website helps Meta verify your business and is recommended. A Facebook page and business documents can also help with verification.

Can I keep using my current number?

It depends on how the number is used today. Message ChatsSync on WhatsApp and we will check the best option for your number.

Is the WhatsApp API free?

Meta charges per delivered template message for some categories, and replies to customers within the 24-hour customer service window are free. Software like ChatsSync is a separate subscription.

What is a WhatsApp message template?

A template is a pre-approved message format you use to start a conversation or message a customer after the 24-hour window. Templates are reviewed by Meta before use.
