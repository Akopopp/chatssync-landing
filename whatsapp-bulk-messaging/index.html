FAQ

Bulk messaging questions

Is WhatsApp bulk messaging allowed?

Yes, when you use the official WhatsApp Business API with Meta-approved message templates and message customers who agreed to hear from you. Unofficial bulk senders break WhatsApp’s terms.

How many messages can I send?

Meta sets a messaging limit for each number, which grows as you send quality messages that customers engage with. ChatsSync shows your campaign results so you can keep quality high.

How much does WhatsApp bulk messaging cost?

Meta charges per delivered template message based on the category (marketing, utility or authentication) and the customer’s country. The ChatsSync plan is separate — see our pricing page.

Is WhatsApp bulk messaging available in Pakistan?

Yes. Pakistani businesses can send bulk WhatsApp campaigns through the official WhatsApp Business API with ChatsSync.

Can I personalise bulk messages?

Yes. Templates support variables, so each customer can see their own name, order number, date or amount.
