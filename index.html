FAQ

Questions, answered

What businesses usually ask us about ChatsSync and the WhatsApp Business API.

What is ChatsSync?

ChatsSync is a WhatsApp CRM built on the official WhatsApp Business API. It gives your team one shared inbox for every customer chat, a rule-based chatbot that answers common questions automatically, bulk campaigns with Meta-approved templates, contacts with labels, reports and an Android app.

Who is ChatsSync for?

Any business that sells or supports customers on WhatsApp — online stores, clinics, real estate agencies, salons, restaurants, schools, travel agents, retailers and service companies. We are based in Pakistan and work with businesses in Pakistan and other countries.

What is the difference between the WhatsApp Business app and the WhatsApp API?

The WhatsApp Business app is made for one person on one phone. The WhatsApp Business API is made for teams: many agents can work on the same number, chatbots can answer automatically, and you can message customers in bulk with approved templates. ChatsSync gives you the API with an easy, WhatsApp-like dashboard.

Can my whole team use one WhatsApp number?

Yes. Your agents all work from the same shared inbox. Chats can be assigned to a person, filtered by Mine, Unassigned or Unread, and agents can leave private notes that customers never see.

Is the ChatsSync chatbot AI?

No. The ChatsSync chatbot is rule-based. You build the flow with buttons, lists, questions and replies, so customers always get the exact answers you approved. A human can take over the chat at any time.

Can I send bulk WhatsApp messages?

Yes. You can send campaigns to your contacts using WhatsApp message templates approved by Meta, and target them by label or segment. Campaign reports show sent, delivered, read and replied.

Will my WhatsApp number get banned?

ChatsSync uses Meta’s official WhatsApp Business API, which is the approved way for businesses to message customers. Numbers usually get restricted because of unofficial bulk tools or spam reports, so we recommend messaging customers who opted in and following WhatsApp’s Business Messaging Policy.

How much does ChatsSync cost?

Rs 5,000 for 1 month, Rs 12,500 for 3 months or Rs 40,000 for 1 year. Every plan includes every feature and 1 WhatsApp number. Extra numbers are charged separately, and Meta’s own template message fees are not included.

Can I connect more than one WhatsApp number?

Yes. Each plan includes 1 WhatsApp number, and you can add more numbers at an additional cost that depends on your needs. Message us on WhatsApp for a quote.

Is the WhatsApp Business API available in Pakistan?

Yes. Businesses in Pakistan can use Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform. ChatsSync helps you connect your number and start messaging customers from the ChatsSync dashboard and Android app. Read our WhatsApp Business API Pakistan guide.

Is there a mobile app?

Yes. The ChatsSync Android app sends instant notifications for new messages so you can reply to customers from your phone.
